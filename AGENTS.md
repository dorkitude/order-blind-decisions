# Repository instructions

- Present provider comparisons as one cohesive study, regardless of collection timing. Integrate every provider into the shared method, result tables, findings and limitations.
- Keep results matter-of-fact. Do not turn READMEs, analyses or result reports into operational diaries, and do not frame providers as additions, extensions, follow-ups or re-runs. Put operational history in issues.
- Collection dates belong in public prose only when they are methodologically relevant provenance.
- Public-facing documentation and results must not name, hint at or contain private providers, unreleased models or internal codenames. Audit case-insensitively before release.
